Career Paths After Class 12

What To Do After 12th?

After completing Class 12, students can pursue undergraduate degrees in Science, Commerce, or Arts. Other options include professional diploma courses, vocational training, or direct job opportunities. Career counseling can help students identify the best path based on their skills and interests.

Popular Career Options After Class 12

Field Common Courses Duration Details
Engineering B.Tech, Diploma in Engineering 4 years (Degree), 3 years (Diploma) Engineering focuses on applying scientific principles to design, build, and maintain structures, machines, and systems. Specializations include Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, and Computer Science.
Medical & Healthcare MBBS, BDS, Nursing, Paramedical Courses 5.5 years (MBBS), 3-4 years (Others) Medical courses prepare students for careers in healthcare. MBBS is for becoming a doctor, while BDS focuses on dentistry. Nursing and paramedical courses train students for supportive roles in healthcare.
Commerce & Business B.Com, BBA, CA, CS 3-5 years Commerce courses focus on accounting, finance, and business management. B.Com is a general degree, while BBA focuses on business administration. CA and CS are professional courses for accounting and corporate law.
Arts & Humanities BA, Mass Communication, Psychology 3 years Arts courses cover a wide range of subjects like literature, history, and sociology. Mass Communication focuses on media and journalism, while Psychology studies human behavior and mental processes.
Law LLB, BA LLB, BBA LLB 3-5 years Law courses prepare students for legal careers. LLB is a traditional law degree, while BA LLB and BBA LLB are integrated courses combining law with arts or business administration.
Design & Creative Arts B.Des, Fashion Design, Animation 3-4 years Design courses focus on creativity and innovation. B.Des covers various design fields, Fashion Design focuses on apparel, and Animation involves creating visual effects and motion graphics.
Information Technology B.Sc IT, BCA, Diploma in IT 3-4 years IT courses focus on software development, programming, and IT infrastructure. B.Sc IT and BCA are degree programs, while Diploma in IT provides practical skills for IT support roles.
Agriculture B.Sc Agriculture, B.Tech Agriculture 4 years Agriculture courses focus on farming techniques, crop management, and agricultural technology. B.Sc Agriculture is a science-based program, while B.Tech Agriculture focuses on engineering applications in farming.
Hotel Management BHM, Diploma in Hotel Management 3-4 years Hotel Management courses train students for careers in hospitality and tourism. BHM is a degree program, while Diploma in Hotel Management provides practical skills for hotel operations.
Aviation B.Sc Aviation, Commercial Pilot Training 3-4 years Aviation courses prepare students for careers in the aviation industry. B.Sc Aviation focuses on aviation science, while Commercial Pilot Training is for aspiring pilots.
Architecture B.Arch, Diploma in Architecture 5 years (B.Arch), 3 years (Diploma) Architecture courses focus on designing buildings and structures. B.Arch is a degree program, while Diploma in Architecture provides foundational skills in architectural design.
Pharmacy B.Pharm, D.Pharm 4 years (B.Pharm), 2 years (D.Pharm) Pharmacy courses focus on the preparation and dispensing of medicines. B.Pharm is a degree program, while D.Pharm is a diploma course for pharmacy technicians.
