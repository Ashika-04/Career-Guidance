Career Paths After Class 10

What To Do After 10th?

After finishing the 10th grade, you might continue your studies in 11th and 12th grades, choosing a field like Science, Commerce, or Arts. Another option is to explore practical courses or diplomas that teach useful skills for jobs. Talk to a career counsellor to understand your strengths and interests. You can also gain work experience through internships or part-time jobs
