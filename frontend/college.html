Courses After 12th

Explore the best career options after 12th in various streams

Science Stream Courses

Students who choose Science can opt for careers in engineering, medicine, research, and technology.

  • Engineering: B.Tech, BE
  • Medical: MBBS, BDS, BAMS
  • Research: B.Sc in Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • Technology: BCA, B.Sc in IT

Commerce Stream Courses

Commerce students can build careers in finance, accounting, business, and economics.

  • Finance & Accounting: B.Com, CA, CS
  • Business: BBA, BBM
  • Economics: BA in Economics
  • Banking & Investment: CFP, CFA

Arts Stream Courses

Arts students can explore careers in humanities, design, media, and education.

  • Humanities: BA in History, Political Science
  • Design: Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA), Fashion Designing
  • Media: Journalism, Mass Communication
  • Education: B.Ed, BA in Psychology
